How we work together

1

Check it over

I look at one area that worries you, such as your AWS account. You get a short written note: what I found, what to fix first, what it should cost, and the saving where there is one. The first call is free.

2

Fix it

I make the changes you choose, test them with your team, and leave a plain handover note. I quote a fixed price before I start, and anything outside the agreed list is priced separately first.

3

Keep it running (optional)

A monthly check, small changes, and a quarterly review so it does not drift back.
