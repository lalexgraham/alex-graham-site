Timesheets & reporting · UK charity

Staff timesheets that fit the existing setup

The problem

A UK charity needed a straightforward way for staff to log their hours.

What I did

Instead of asking them to buy new software, I built it on the Excel, SharePoint and Power Apps they were already using.

What changed

Time worked can be logged straight in Microsoft Teams. Data is saved inside the existing systems, so there's no new software, no extra login and a familiarity with the Microsoft ecosystem.

The result

Staff can log their hours in a few clicks, and the charity can see the data in real time. It saves time and money, and is easier for everyone.
