Good technology for organisations that do good work

Charities, CICs and not-for-profits often cannot afford a consultancy and cannot afford to get technology wrong. I do this alongside my main work, so places are limited, but I do make time.

Tell me what you need

The kind of problems I fix

  • Timesheets, rotas, case logs and volunteer records that live in spreadsheets and email.
  • Reports for funders and trustees that take days to produce.
  • Data protection and access: who can see what, and what must be deleted.
  • A system that only one person understands.
  • Whether AI could help, and how to use it safely.

What you can expect

  • A fixed price and a clear list of what is and is not included.
  • Work built on tools you already have wherever possible.
  • Delivered on time, on budget, and explained in plain English.
  • No upselling. If you do not need me, I will say so.

A recent example

A Birmingham charity needed staff timesheets that met HMRC rules. I built them an app using Microsoft PowerApps inside the Microsoft ecosystem they already used, so there was no new software and no extra logins and they can take ownership, so they don’t need to rely on and pay for people like me.
